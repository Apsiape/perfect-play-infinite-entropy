\section*{How this work was done}\label{sec:methods}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How this work was done}

The authors selected the questions and proof standards, directed the research, and take responsibility for every claim. AI systems from more than one developer assisted with exploration, derivation, programming and drafting.

The mathematical evidence consists of the proofs in this paper and the explicitly cited external theorems. The ancillary programs provide reproducible finite checks. Every theorem passed at least one independent check by a different model family, with its own exact computation. The two Bell evaluators and two game evaluators use separate implementations; their provenance and scope are stated in Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}. Independent checks of Theorem~D include exact scalar values and constants and a literal full-functional evaluation on filtered states. The latter reuses the cross-checked Bell measurement builder but independently constructs the filtered state, contracts the coefficients and compares the result with \eqref{eq:filteredloss}.

Finite exact evaluations test the exported predicates, coefficient tables, projection identities and cutoff boundary formulas. They do not prove infinite-dimensional attainment, universal entropy divergence, or a robustness theorem by sampling. The asymptotic conclusions rest on the written proofs. This preprint has not been peer reviewed.
