\subsection{Height cutoffs and the logarithmic upper bound}\label{sec:v11-height}
This subsection gives the height-and-size construction proving the upper bound in Theorem~D.

Choose the unitary polar completions in Section~\ref{sec:assembly} as follows. For $B$, cycle each chain $r,rB,\ldots,rB^{h-1}$, where $r$ ends in $A$ and has height $h$, and fix the empty word. For the amplified $a$, interchange $e_0\otimes w$ and $e_2\otimes wA$, fixing the complement. The cycle preserves word size and maximum prefix height. The swap preserves the scaffold weight because $4\cdot4^{-(n+1)}=4^{-n}$. Both are permitted centralizer polar completions. Transport the same choices along the two bridges.

For a prefix-closed, $B$-closed set $S$, write
\[
 Z_S=\sum_{w\in S}4^{-n(w)},\quad M_S=\sum_{w\in S}n(w)4^{-n(w)},\quad
 b_S=\sum_{\substack{w\in S\\wA\notin S}}4^{-n(w)},\quad q_S=b_S/Z_S.
\]
The coefficient operator of the scaffold state restricted to $S$ is diagonal, with entries
\[
 K_S(f,c,w)=\sqrt{d_c4^{-n(w)}/(75Z_S)},\qquad
 (d_c)=(1,2,2,2,2,4,4,4,4).
\]
For a binary projection $E$, its synchronization loss is $\|EK_S-K_SE\|_2^2$.
The $B$ cycles reduce $S$. Only a swap whose parent is in the $A$ boundary crosses its support. The finite word list contains no label mixing an $a$ token and a $B$ token; labels with two $a$ tokens preserve histories. All core rows are still zero operator identities, and all old FMS and refinement losses vanish. Thus
\[
 \ell_B(S)=\sum_E\kappa_E\sigma_E,\qquad
 \kappa_E=1+\sum_{r:E\in r}\left(1+\tfrac12\sum_{F\ne I}\alpha_{r,F}^2\right).
\]
The exact symbolic crossing calculation in the verification bundle gives 392 active symbols: 78 $G$, 78 $H$, 156 complements and 80 triangles. On these symbols the range of $\kappa_E$ is $[7/2,221/2]$. Summation gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v11-boundary}
 \ell_B(S)=\Lambda_Bq_S,\quad
 \ell_G(S)=\Lambda_Gq_S/Q^2,\quad
 \Lambda_B=\frac{50808529}{540000},\quad
 \Lambda_G=\frac{14844029}{54000}.
\end{equation}
For the game, each old symbol contributes $49\sigma_E$, each nonzero row slot contributes another $3\sigma_E$, and column synchronization and graph orthogonality supply the remaining terms. The old sign-event formula $\ell_G=\ell_B/Q^2$ is specific to radial filters and is not used here. The exact phase calculation checks arbitrary unit-modulus counter phases, not only selected heights. The bundle records the exact rational crossing certificate, its independent implementation and the arbitrary-phase audit.

Let $S_H$ consist of histories whose every prefix has height at most $H$. The mass at current height $h$ is
\[
 m_h=\frac{H+1-h}{(H+1)2^h},\qquad 0\le h\le H.
\]
Indeed $m_0=1$, $m_h=m_{h-1}/4+m_{h+1}$ and $m_{H+1}=0$; the repeated-root recurrence gives the displayed solution. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v11-height-formulas}
 Z_H=\frac{2H+2^{-H}}{H+1},\quad b_H=\frac{2^{-H}}{H+1},\quad
 q_H=\frac1{H2^{H+1}+1}.
\end{equation}
For completeness the moment can be calculated without enumerating histories. Let $T_h(x)$ count continuations from height $h$ and put $Z_H(x)=1+xT_1(x)$. Then
\[
 T_h=1+xT_{h+1}+\mathbf1_{h\ge2}T_{h-1},\qquad T_{H+1}=0.
\]
If $Y_h=xT'_h(x)$, differentiation gives the same linear system with inhomogeneous term $xT_{h+1}$. Solving at $x=1/4$ gives
\[
 M_H=xZ'_H(x)\big|_{1/4}
 =\frac{2H(H-1)}{3(H+1)}+\frac{H(H+5)2^{-H}}{6(H+1)}.
\]
The equations uniquely determine the solution because the finite-strip transfer matrix has spectral radius less than one. These formulas can also be verified by substitution in the two linear systems.
Including the finite scaffold, the marginal entropy is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v11-height-entropy}
 S_B(H)=\log_2 75-\frac85+\log_2Z_H+\frac{2M_H}{Z_H},\qquad S_G(H)=S_B(H)+8.
\end{equation}
Write $q=2^{-H}$ and
\[
 \frac{M_H}{Z_H}=\frac{H-1}{3}+\delta_H,\quad
 \delta_H=\frac{q(H+1)(H+2)}{6(2H+q)},\quad
 c_\infty=\log_2 150-\frac85-\frac23.
\]
The inequality $2^{-H}(H+1)^2(H+2)\le6H$ follows at $H=1$ by equality and then by comparing successive ratios. Hence $2\delta_H\le1/(H+1)$, whereas
\[
 -\log_2(Z_H/2)\ge\frac{2-q}{2(H+1)\ln2}>\frac1{H+1}.
\]
It follows that $S_B(H)\le2H/3+c_\infty$ and $S_B(H)=2H/3+c_\infty+o(1)$.

Here is an integer-rounding check for the refined Bell constant. Choose the least $H$ with $\Lambda_Bq_H<\varepsilon$. If $\varepsilon\le\Lambda_Bq_7$, then $H\ge8$. Put $a=\log_2\Lambda_B$, $x=H-1$, and $L=\log_2(1/(\Lambda_Bq_{H-1}))$. The function $y-\log_2y$ is increasing for $y>1/\ln2$, so the worst endpoint of the interval is its upper-loss endpoint. Furthermore
\[
 \frac Lx=1+\frac{1+\log_2x-a+e_x}{x},\qquad
 0<e_x=\log_2(1+(x2^{x+1})^{-1})<0.001.
\]
The maximum of $(\log_2x+1.001-a)/x$ over positive real $x$ is
$1/(\ln2\,2^{a-1.001+1/\ln2})<0.0114$. Therefore
\[
 S_B(H)-\frac23L+\frac23\log_2 L
 \le c_\infty+\frac23a+\frac23\log_2(1.0114)<9.35.
\]
With the fixed game normalization the same calculation changes only the additive constant. In particular the construction gives
\begin{align*}
 E_B(\varepsilon)&\le\frac23\log_2(1/\varepsilon)-\frac23\log_2\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+9.35,\\
 E_G(\varepsilon)&\le\frac23\log_2(1/\varepsilon)-\frac23\log_2\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+O(1).
\end{align*}
Equation~\eqref{eq:v11-boundary} is an exact rational computation checked by two implementations; it affects only the constant in these upper bounds, not their order. The lower bound is proved by the decoding and rounding appendices.

\paragraph{Finite-dimensional implementation.}
The pure height cutoff has infinite Schmidt rank for every $H\ge2$: all words $A(AB)^k$ survive. It must not be called a finite-dimensional strategy. Instead use
\[
 S_{H,N}=\{w:\max_{v\preceq w}\operatorname{ht}(v)\le H,\ n(w)\le N\}.
\]
This is a finite, prefix-closed, $B$-closed set. At fixed $H$ its mass and first moment converge to $Z_H,M_H$ exponentially in $N$; the finite-strip transfer eigenvalue is $\cos^2(\pi/(H+1))<1$ for $H\ge2$, and $H=1$ is already finite. Its boundary ratio also converges to $q_H$. Compress each full measurement to the finite Schmidt support; positivity and the POVM sums are preserved, as are all probabilities on that state. The strict loss slack in the choice of $H$ and the strict constant slack above allow a finite $N$. Explicitly, enumerate increasing $N$ until the rational loss is below $\varepsilon$ and the entropy from $Z_{H,N},M_{H,N}$ is below the displayed bound. Convergence guarantees termination. The exact height-only loss formula is not asserted for this additional cutoff.

\paragraph{The explicit $S_4$ witness.}
Starting from $S_4$, forbid the $A$ edge at surviving height-three words with $n(w)\ge18$. At $u_0=AAAABABABA$, which has seven $A$ letters and height four, add the $A$ child and all its continuations of height at most five. The added subtree uses that height-five rule rather than the pruning rule. Exact finite-strip summation gives
\[
 Z'=\frac{55409916813}{34359738368},\quad
 M'=\frac{57550635573}{34359738368},\quad
 b'=\frac{1288366711}{103079215104}.
\]
Thus $q'=1288366711/166229750439<1/129=q_4$ and the history entropy decreases from $2.7668185404$ to $2.7666935844$ bits. Fixed scaffold factors add the same entropy to both states. This checked witness rules out exact optimality of pure height caps; it does not identify the unrestricted entropy frontier.
